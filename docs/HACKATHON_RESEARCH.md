## Screening and scored judging

Stage one checks viability, fit and reasonable Serverpod use on a pass/fail basis. AI assistance checks completeness and prepares summaries; every entry that is not clearly incomplete receives human review before stage-one failure. Automated tooling neither scores nor chooses winners. Human judges score eligible entries. [Official Rules, §6 p.5](</home/shiva396/Documents/temp/serverpod hackathon rules.pdf>)

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence to make visible |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Working behavior | 30% | Core flow completes with real functionality |
| Serverpod contribution | 25% | Backend performs substantive work |
| Craft and technical creativity | 25% | Thoughtful implementation, coherent UX and sound technical choices |
| Usefulness | 20% | Clear user, problem and practical benefit |

Ties compare criteria in this order, then use a judges' vote. [Official Rules, §6 p.6](</home/shiva396/Documents/temp/serverpod hackathon rules.pdf>)

## All prizes and optional bonus entries

All amounts are USD; overall awards are per team, not per member. Credits are Serverpod Cloud credits. [Official Rules, §8 p.7](</home/shiva396/Documents/temp/serverpod hackathon rules.pdf>)

| Award | Cash | Cloud credits |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| First | $5,000 | $2,500 |
| Second | $3,000 | $2,000 |
| Third | $1,000 | $1,500 |
| Fourth | $500 | $1,500 |
| Fifth | — | $1,500 |
| Most Valuable Feedback | $500 | $500 |
| Best Hackathon Post | — | $500 |
| Total | $10,000 | $10,000 |

A project can win one overall prize; an overall winner may also win Best Hackathon Post. Feedback is awarded to individuals: one complete, actionable feedback form per entrant, registration and an eligible project submission required. Section 8 contains wording about feedback-only entrants that is awkward beside section 4's explicit eligible-project requirement; the safe preparation is to submit an eligible project as well. Feedback and public posts are **optional extras**, not prerequisites for the main competition. [Official Rules, §4 p.3; §8 p.7](</home/shiva396/Documents/temp/serverpod hackathon rules.pdf>)

For the post prize, a registered entrant publishes during the window, clearly identifies the event, follows platform terms/conduct and shows interesting/useful real work. Multiple posts allowed, best considered; sponsor chooses, no vote, and may withhold the award. [Official Rules, §6 p.6](</home/shiva396/Documents/temp/serverpod hackathon rules.pdf>)

Prizes depend on identity/qualification/contribution verification. Required forms are due within ten business days of issue; prizes delivered within thirty days after completed forms arrive. Cash is paid by bank transfer; Cloud credits are non-transferable and valid one year. Winners handle taxes, banking/currency fees and applicable reporting; sanctions/payment restrictions can affect delivery. [Official Rules, §8 p.8](</home/shiva396/Documents/temp/serverpod hackathon rules.pdf>)

## Eligibility, ownership and post-deadline handling

Eligible individuals must be the age of majority in their residence at entry. Teams have at most four; existing legal organizations may enter; teams/organizations designate an authorized representative. Individuals may enter personally and on multiple teams, and multiple projects must be unique and substantially different. [Official Rules, §3 p.2; §4 p.4](</home/shiva396/Documents/temp/serverpod hackathon rules.pdf>)

Excluded residences/domiciles include Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Belarus, Crimea/non-government-controlled Ukraine, Cuba, Iran, North Korea and Syria, plus other prohibited/sanctioned territories. Promotion entities, their employees/agents/families/households/affiliates, judges and judges' employers, other event participants in organizing roles and conflicting interests are also excluded. Sponsor/administrator financially or preferentially supported projects can be disqualified. No blanket eligibility certification follows from these rules or the user's timezone. [Official Rules, §3 p.2; §4 p.5](</home/shiva396/Documents/temp/serverpod hackathon rules.pdf>)

Entrants retain project/code ownership. The organizer receives a twelve-month nonexclusive license for judging, event administration and promotion/reporting, not commercialization. Organizer-created private repositories are archived after judging and can subsequently be transferred/deleted on request. [Official Rules, §7 pp.6–7](</home/shiva396/Documents/temp/serverpod hackathon rules.pdf>)

Submitted materials cannot change after the deadline; development may continue in the entrant's own repository. Limited organizer-approved changes to rights/privacy/inappropriate content are possible if substance stays the same. Preserve a tagged release/commit and judge-accessible build so continued work does not replace the submitted behavior; this preservation method is an audit recommendation. [Official Rules, §5 p.5](</home/shiva396/Documents/temp/serverpod hackathon rules.pdf>)

Results may publish entrant/team names, title, summary and selected demonstration videos. Distinct advertising/marketing use requires separate optional consent; mailing-list opt-in is optional. Conduct rules apply to event/community activity. Swedish substantive law and courts are named; BuilderBase platform terms and applicable Serverpod/AI service licenses also apply. [Official Rules, §10 pp.9–10; §§13–16 pp.11–12](</home/shiva396/Documents/temp/serverpod hackathon rules.pdf>)
